### 🪴 Power BI
<br>

The Excel model proves the logic works, but it still requires someone to open the file, trace the formulas, and manually flip the scenario inputs. Power BI turns that same model into something leadership can explore on their own, so clicking a scenario, see the cash impact, no spreadsheet literacy required.  
<br>

**🌱 How I used Power BI**
- Rebuilt the Excel scenario logic as live DAX measures, so the 13-week forecast recalculates in real time instead of staying frozen at whatever scenario was last saved
- Best / Base / Worst scenario switching (via slicer) shows how collection delays and payment stretching actually move the cash position, not just the assumptions behind them
- Waterfall visualization shows exactly when and why cash position changes week-to-week
- Top AR/AP overdue rankings surface the specific invoices and vendors driving risk. But AP overdue is read carefully here, since it can reflect deliberate cash management or quality-related withholding rather than distress
